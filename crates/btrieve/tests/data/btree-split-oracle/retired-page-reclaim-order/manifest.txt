reclen 12, pagesize 512, unique 4-byte key. Built a 7-leaf tree (insert
1..152 ascending), then retired two WELL-SEPARATED leaves independently
(each via delete-its-own-top-3-keys: one redistribution, then a merge on
the second underflow -- the same recipe underflow-lifecycle-512 uses).

1-none-retired.dat / 2-leaf10-retired.dat: deleting leaf 10's own top two
  keys (65, then 64) retires it -- FCR+0x98 (INDEX_FREE_V6) becomes
  0x0000000a (10).
3-before-leaf12-retirement.dat / 4-both-retired.dat: deleting leaf 12's
  own top two keys retires IT too -- FCR+0x98 becomes 0x0000000c (12),
  and logical-12's own retired page body (offset 2..6, word-swapped long)
  now holds 10 -- the free list is a genuine LIFO chain: head -> 12 ->
  10 -> NOWHERE.
5-before-first-reclaim.dat / 6-leaf12-reclaimed-first.dat: enough more
  ascending inserts (to key 155) to force a fresh split -- logical 12
  (the chain HEAD, most recently retired) is the one reclaimed. FCR+0x98
  updates to 0x0000000a (10) -- the chain's OWN next-hop becomes the new
  head, exactly like popping a stack.
7-before-second-reclaim.dat / 8-leaf10-reclaimed-second.dat: further
  inserts (to key 177) force another split -- logical 10 (now the only
  remaining retired page) is reclaimed, FCR+0x98 returns to NOWHERE.

Finding: reclamation is LIFO (pulls from the free list's HEAD, i.e. the
MOST RECENTLY retired page) -- not FIFO (list tail) and not lowest-
logical-id-first (12 was reclaimed before 10 despite 12 > 10). Matches
`docs/2026-08-25-btree-split-rules.md`'s own description of the same
mechanism (built independently by the concurrent implementer from this
same class of recording) and `crates/btrieve/src/pages.rs`'s
`fcr::INDEX_FREE_V6` constant / `V6Page::retired` field, added since round
1 of this doc specifically to support tag `0x4500`.
