The rule this settles: "right sibling first" (docs' round 2 finding) does
not stop applying just because the cascade has already consumed the
formal right sibling -- whichever leaf IS currently rightmost still only
has its left neighbour to ask, and that ask is a redistribution or a merge
by the EXACT SAME threshold as everywhere else in this doc: redistribute
if the neighbour has MORE than half_entries (safe to donate one and stay
at or above the minimum), merge if the neighbour is AT half_entries
(donating would put it below the minimum too).
